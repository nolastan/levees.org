Counties in U.S. protected by levees (click to enlarge)

Today Insurance Journal magazine published Levees.org’s levee statistics and new data in its periodical.

Insurance Journal and its web site, products of Wells Publishing, is a trade news organization covering the property/casualty insurance industry. Its magazines are published on a national/regional basis 24 times a year. There are five regional editions, and around 43,000 subscribers.

The levee county data for the research was attained by Levees.org in a request to FEMA under the Freedom of Information Act. The research was conducted by geographer Ezra Boyd.
