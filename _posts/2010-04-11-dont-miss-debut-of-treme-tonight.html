If you haven’t invited friends over (or invited yourself to a friend’s place) to watch the series premiere of Treme, there’s still time!

Tonight at 9:00 CST, HBO’s new series will debut, and rumor has it that Levees.org supporters will applaud the character played by John Goodman. I have it on good authority that he has some choice words for the Army Corps of Engineers.

Mr. Goodman of course, is a spokesperson for Levees.org and volunteered his time to appear in a public service announcement for Levees.org in 2007.

And according to New Orleans Times Picayune reporter Dave Walker, “…with a few notable exceptions, reviews (for Treme) so far from outlets of all sizes all over the country have been favorable unto near-delirious with praise…”

Don’t miss the show tonight!
