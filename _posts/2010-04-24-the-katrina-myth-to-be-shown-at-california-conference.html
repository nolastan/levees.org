The Katrina Myth, design by Blake Haney

The Katrina Myth, our 10-minute video on the truth about the New Orleans flooding will be played before the National Conference for Catholic Facility Management (CCFM) in Costa Mesa, California in early May.

CCFM is a national organization of facility and real estate managers for Catholic dioceses and religious orders and meets annually at various locations across the country.

Levees.org is pleased because that is exactly why the video was produced, to help spread the word. It’s especially fitting that the video be played in California. According to Dr. Ray Seed at the University of California Berkeley, the state of California “has about a quarter of the nation’s flood risk, more than Louisiana and the next several states combined.”
