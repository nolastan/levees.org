June 9-11, the John Jay College of Criminal Justice will host a conference in New Orleans on Critical Incidents Analysis: Displacement as an Obstacle to Recovery at the Superbowl Holiday Inn.

Jed Horne, author of Breach of Faith, will be keynote speaker at the conference’s commencement on June 9th. Levees.org, one of the planners of the conference, will lead a levee breach tour on Thursday June 10th. Supporters of Levees.org have generously volunteered their time and vehicles to transport the conference guests for the tour.

Sandy Rosenthal, founder of Levees.org will speak on Thursday.
