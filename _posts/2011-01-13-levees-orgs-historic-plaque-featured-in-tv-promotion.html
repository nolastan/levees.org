When Levees.org installed a Historic Plaque at the 17th Street Canal breach site, our hope was for everyone to see it.

The Historic Plaque was our gift to the residents of the Lakeview neighborhood, and we wanted the true facts at Ground Zero to be there for all to see.

So we were quite pleased to learn that the beautiful Historic Plaque is featured in WGNO TV’s high quality promotional video.

Thank you WGNO!
