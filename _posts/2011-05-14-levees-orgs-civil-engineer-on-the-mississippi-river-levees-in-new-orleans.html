Positioned as the go-to for information about levees, Levees.org has been queried repeatedly by news meda as well as worried residents.

This week, we talked to national media including ABC News and CNN and local media including WWL Radio and Fox News 8.

We have selected H.J. Bosworth Jr, civil engineer and senior member of Levees.org as the best person to address questions regarding the Mississippi River flooding, and exactly what that means to residents in the New Orleans area.

We are making this Fox 8 News story by John Snell available for anyone wanting information the Mississippi River levees.
