Harry Shearer, director of The Big Uneasy

If not tabled due to Hurricane Irene coverage, Harry Shearer, producer of The Big Uneasy, will be featured on CBS Sunday Morning News today August 28 to talk about the documentary.

Other news:   The Big Uneasy is now available on DVD or download at Amazon, iTunes, Vudu and other outlets.

The more people who see The Big Uneasy the better. The 96-minute movie explains what really happened when greater New Orleans flooded and why.

And that’s important because 55% of the American people live in counties protected by levees.
