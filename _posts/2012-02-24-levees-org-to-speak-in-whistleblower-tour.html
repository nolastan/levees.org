The American Whistleblower Tour is making a stop in New Orleans this week and Levees.org is featured.

Attendance is FREE and open to the public.

When: Tuesday, February 28th, 4:00-5:30 p.m.
Where: Lavin-Bernick Center, Kendall Cram Room, Tulane University

Parking on street is free on downtown side of campus for 2 hours.  On downtown side of State Street, street parking is free and unlimited.

Panelists will discuss how whistleblowers promote transparency and accountability in key issues facing local New Orleans area residents.

This event is sponsored by the Government Accountability Project (GAP), Tulane University, and the New Orleans Coalition on Open Governance (NOCOG).

A wine and cheese reception will follow the event.

For more event info, please visit here: http://bit.ly/z4uuXN
