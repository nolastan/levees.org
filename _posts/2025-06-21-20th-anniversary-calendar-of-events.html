Guided Levee Breach Tour

August 23, 2025, 9-10:00am • 5000 Warrington Drive, New Orleans

Visitors will receive a guided tour of the Levee Exhibit Hall and Flooded House Museum from Sandy Rosenthal at the London Avenue breach. Note: no A/C nor bathroom facilities.

Book Signing at Octavia Book Store

August 27, 2025, 6-7:30pm • 513 Octavia Street, New Orleans

Founder Sandy Rosenthal will read from and sign the special anniversary edition of her book Words Whispered in Water. There will be wine and cheese.

Documentary Screening

August 28, 2025, 6-7:00pm • 160 West Campus Drive, Destrehan

Join Sandy and Historian & Documentarian Royd Anderson who will screen Leading Ladies of Hurricane Katrina at the East Regional Library in Destrehan.
