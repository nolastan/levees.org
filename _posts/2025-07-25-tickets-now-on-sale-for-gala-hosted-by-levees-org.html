Front cover of Gala program, Aug 9, 2025

Levees.org will commemorate the 20th Anniversary of Hurricane Katrina with a Gala featuring Lt. Gen Russel Honore who brought order to New Orleans after the levees broke.

August 9, 2025 from 7:00 – 10:00p
The Cannery, 3803 Toulouse Street

Delicious food, live music, presentations, highlights and “greatest hits” are all included in the $25 ticket price. A good buy made possible by sponsors.

The first 150 registrants receive a free first-edition of Words Whispered in Water: Why the Levees Broke in Hurricane Katrina. This first edition will be retired as of August 26, when a special anniversary edition will be released.
