Fox Weather documentary

Fox News Media’s free streaming weather network Fox Weather will debut a multipart documentary on Hurricane Katrina in late August, timed to coincide with the 20-year anniversary of the storm.

The first installment will air on August 25, and revists when Hurricane Katrina made landfall in New Orleans. Fox Weather hurricane specialist Bryan Norcross, correspondent Robert Ray will cover the chain of events that followed, including federal levee failures and critical decisions that turned a natural disaster into an urban catastrophe.

Founder Sandy Rosenthal and Levees.org’s Flooded House Museum will be featured in the fourth and final episode.

Fox Weather is available on most popular free, ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) platforms, including Plex, Pluto TV, The Roku Channel, Xumo Play, Samsung TV Plus, Sling Freestream, Vizio WatchFree Plus and LG Channels.
