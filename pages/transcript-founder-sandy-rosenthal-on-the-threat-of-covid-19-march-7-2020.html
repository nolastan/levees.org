On March 7, 2020, Levees.org founder Sandy Rosenthal was an invited guest speaker at Tulane University’s annual conference, Women Making Waves. Rosenthal spoke on “Leading in a Time of Disaster.” The night before the conference, Rosenthal decided to use the platform to discuss the threat of COVID-19 to the New Orleans community. Below is a transcript of those remarks.

Coronovirus is here, and likely on this campus right now.

Denial won’t make it go away. 

Just like packing for 2 days for Hurricane Katrina won’t magically result in a 2-day vacation. 

When I packed for Hurricane Katrina, I packed for three weeks.

My zealous preparation put me in a relatively protected space while news about a drowned New Orleans population rocketed around the world.

My zealous preparation allowed me to observe the levee breach disaster with laser focus.

Most importantly, my zealous preparation allowed me to observe the botched federal response with eyes wide open.

Soon afterward, I founded the group Levees.org which exposed the federal government’s levee building mistake and its scandalous efforts to cover it up.

Fifteen years later, for reasons that will one day be the subject of books, the U.S. government did nothing while COVID-19 spread throughout the globe.

While the U.S. did nothing, I got myself prepared. 

Three weeks ago, I stocked up on supplies: cleaners, paper products, cold medicines, protective gear, non-perishables, plus a few of my favorite foods (Hersey Bars). 

So now here I am.

COVID-19 is knocking at the door, and I am reliving my experience of 15 years ago. 

I am reliving a zealous preparation. 

I am reliving a disaster unfolding.

And I am reliving a botched government response.

COVID-19 is here and cannot be stopped.

But we can lesson the spread.

We can avoid touching others. I prefer the Thai “wei’. 

And we can avoid touching our faces unless our hands are squeaky clean. 

COVID-19 is not a Stephen King-esque virus.

But it’s here. 

And you can protect yourselves and your families. 

Do it now.

––Sandy Rosenthal is founder of Levees.org and author of Words Whispered in Water: Why the Levees Broke in Hurricane Katrina (Mango Publishing, due out August 2020 as audiobook and paperback)
